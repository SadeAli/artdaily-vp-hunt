A little city photographed through a real lens: one horizon, two vanishing points. Drag the dashed guide onto the hidden horizon and park a ⊕ marker on each VP, then lock it in — three scenes per round. The ⊕s ride the line, because in two-point perspective both VPs sit on the horizon: slide a ⊕ sideways to move that VP, pull it up or down and the whole horizon comes along.

Rather work like you would at the easel? Press trace edges and draw along two receding edges of the same wall — where your two strokes cross is that vanishing point. Two strokes per VP, and the horizon comes free. Undo pops the last stroke; nothing you draw costs points.

Keys: ← → slide the selected ⊕, ↑ ↓ raise and lower the horizon, shift for fine steps, space swaps ⊕, enter locks in.

Scoring: horizon placement is 40%, each vanishing point 30%. Full credit inside a small bullseye (about 2% of the frame), fading to zero at 12% of the height for the horizon and 15% of the width for a VP.

Studio tip: pick two edges of the same wall and extend them — where they kiss is the VP, and the horizon runs level through it.
